Usage: __ME__ jobs [WORKER] [--json] [-w | --watch] [-n SECONDS | --interval SECONDS]

List registered jobs. With a worker name, print its job, topic, state and
start time columns. Without a worker name, list jobs on reachable workers,
with a worker column in front. When the output is not a terminal, the columns
are [worker,] job, state, start time and topic, separated by tabs. --json
returns objects. The topic is what start --topic gave the job, or the
worker's topic for the job of exec.

Watch refreshes as soon as the controller reports a job event, and at least
every 5 seconds by default. Press q to quit or another key to refresh
immediately. --json --watch emits one JSON array per refresh.
