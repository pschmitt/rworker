Usage: __ME__ sync [--wait [SECONDS]] [USER@]NAME SRC [DEST]

rsync the directory SRC to the worker (DEST defaults to SRC's basename in
the user's home). Mirrors with --delete and skips target/, .direnv/ and
result.

--wait retries until the worker accepts SSH; optional SECONDS sets a timeout.
Without a timeout, interrupt it with Ctrl-C.
