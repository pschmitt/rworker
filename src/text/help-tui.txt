Usage: __ME__ tui [VIEW] [-n|--interval SECONDS]

An interactive terminal UI: live worker inventory, the jobs of a worker,
their logs and the
lifecycle events, followed live, and the actions on the selected worker
(ssh, inspect, ps, extend, topic, claim, down, new worker, ...).
VIEW is workers (default), jobs, profiles, events or logs. Use any non-empty
prefix, such as prof for profiles or j for jobs.
In Profiles, select a row and press Enter or i for its image or runner details.
Press J to toggle Workers and Jobs, or Enter for the selected worker’s jobs.
Press h to show or hide retired workers inline.
Press c to open the worker creation form, or C to claim the selected worker.
Press n to toggle plain view in all views. In Jobs, press c to start a job.
Alt+Shift+Q quits from any view.
The second pane follows the selected worker’s logs. Click a row to
select it, double-click it to press Enter on it, or scroll over a table
or log pane.
: runs any other action (sync, cp, exec, setup, ...). ? opens help for the current view.
F1 opens help while editing a field.

  -n SECONDS, --interval SECONDS
                 jobs refresh and inventory polling fallback interval (default 5);
                 job lists also refresh at once on job events
