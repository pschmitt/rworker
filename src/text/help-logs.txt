Usage: __ME__ logs [WORKER [JOB]] [--lines N] [--redact] [--redact-regex REGEX] [-g|--filter|--grep REGEX]... [--follow] [--no-events] [--quiet] [--topic REGEX] [--agent REGEX] [--agent-id REGEX] [--plain]
       __ME__ logs [-f] [--topic REGEX] [--agent REGEX] [--agent-id REGEX]  # follow jobs and events on all (or matching) workers
       __ME__ logs WORKER [JOB] --cached [--lines N] [--redact] [--redact-regex REGEX]

With --follow in a terminal, logs opens the TUI with just this log, without
its chrome: the same output, but q or Esc leaves it for the worker list, J
shows the jobs (of this worker, or all of them), / filters, w toggles
wrapping and n toggles the chrome. --plain prints the plain stream instead,
as logs always does into a pipe. Snapshots (without --follow) print and exit.

For WORKER [JOB] snapshots, fall back to the archive when live logs are unavailable.
Use --cached to read only the archive.

--redact masks common credential formats locally. --redact-regex also masks
matches of an expression. Saved logs are kept verbatim.

--filter REGEX (--grep, -g) shows only lines matching the case-insensitive
expression and highlights the matches, keeping the lines' own colors such
as the worker/job prefixes. Repeat it to show lines matching any of them;
it works with --follow too, e.g. `__ME__ logs -f -g error -g warning`.
